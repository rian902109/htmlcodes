Cheetah

Type: Big Cat

live in:open savannas, grasslands, and semi-arid plains of sub-Saharan Africa

Diet : antelopes, impalas , warthogs and hares

Cheetahs are the fastest land animals, capable of reaching speeds up to \(70\) to \(112\) km/h, and they can accelerate from \(0\) to \(60\) mph in just three seconds
